- `getHeight()` without a token works today. That may change; the docs say every HyperSync request needs a token.
- The docs' "Running locally" page says `envio start -r` for local restarts. The migration guide and CLI say `envio dev -r`. The skill follows the CLI.
- The envio.dev/chains pages show V2 config (`rpc_config`, `id: arc`). We flagged this as stale because 3.14.0 rejects both.
- The Free-plan limits (3 indexers, 30-day lifespan, 100k events / 5 GB / 7 days soft limits) are copied from the docs and may change.
- The prize-track wording ("may help with Arc, World, Uniswap Foundation, Hedera") is our judgement. Envio itself is not on the official Mumbai track list.
